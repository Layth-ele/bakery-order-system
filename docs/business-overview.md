# 💼 Delight Bakehouse - Business Overview

## Executive Summary

Delight Bakehouse is a comprehensive, cloud-native order management platform specifically designed for wholesale bakery operations. Built with modern web technologies and Firebase, this SaaS solution streamlines the entire bakery business workflow from customer onboarding to order fulfillment.

**Market Opportunity:** The global bakery market is valued at $500B+ annually, with wholesale operations representing 40% of total market value. Digital transformation in food service is accelerating, with 70% of businesses planning technology investments in 2024.

**Product Value:** Complete bakery management system with customer lifecycle management, order processing, financial tracking, and business intelligence - all in one integrated platform.

**Revenue Model:** SaaS subscription with tiered pricing based on business size and feature requirements.
